**Paws & Mischief**

**Tales of a Tail Wagging Troublemaker**
*By Joanne Maddison · Illustrated by Isabella Parker*




{{< image-caption src="/images/71gyayfcazl._sl1500_.jpg" >}}Paws & Mischief - Tales of a Tail-Wagging Troublemaker{{< /image-caption >}}

Hola,

Pull up a lounger for this one.

Meet Snoopy, the pint-sized rescue dog who crash-landed into our lives straight from a little town in Andalucía.

His previous owner, a lovely lady who had fallen ill, simply couldn’t keep him. A friend who lived in the mountains began a campaign of daily dog emojis, cute photos, and pleading texts: “He’s tiny! He loves kids! Look at that face!”

We lasted about a week before caving.
 “Fine,” we said. “How much trouble can one small dog be?”

(Spoiler: a lot. The best kind.)

Somewhere between the chaos and the cuddles — the puddles, the supermarket escapes, the mountain, the school-run splash — this little Spanish dog completely rearranged our home and our hearts.

*Paws & Mischief* is the true story of how one tiny dog from Andalucía taught us that the best kind of trouble is the kind that makes life bigger, louder, and full of unexpected joy.

It’s a short illustrated book (Isabella did the drawings). Perfect for little ones, dog lovers, or anyone who’s ever lived with a small dog who was absolutely convinced they ran the house.
