Javea (Xàbia) is one of the most beautiful and popular towns on the Costa Blanca North. With its dramatic coastline, crystal-clear waters, charming old town, and lively port, it offers a fantastic mix of natural beauty and local character.

However, where you choose to stay in Javea can make a big difference to your experience — whether you’re visiting for the first time, staying long-term, or travelling with a dog.

Here’s a practical guide to the best areas, based on real experience.

{{< image-caption src="/images/sun-sea-sangra-javeaxabia-2-.png" >}}Javea Arenal Beach{{< /image-caption >}}



**1. Jávea Old Town (Casco Antiguo)**

The historic old town sits slightly inland and is full of narrow streets, traditional houses, small squares, and local shops and restaurants. It has a strong Spanish feel and is especially atmospheric in the evenings.

**Best for:** People who want character, history, and a more local atmosphere.

**Pros:**

* Authentic Spanish feel
* Good selection of local restaurants and bars
* Quieter in the evenings
* Easy to walk around

**Cons:**

* Further from the beaches (10–20 minute walk or short drive)
* Limited parking in the centre

**Good choice if** you prefer a traditional town feel over being right on the beach.

{{< image-caption src="/images/sun-sea-sangria-javea-5-.png" >}}Javea Old Town{{< /image-caption >}}



**2. Puerto de Javea (The Port)**

The port area is lively and popular, with a working marina, plenty of restaurants, bars, and shops along the waterfront. It has a relaxed, slightly international feel and is very convenient.

**Best for:** First-time visitors, food lovers, and people who enjoy being near restaurants and the sea.

**Pros:**

* Lots of restaurants and bars within walking distance
* Beautiful marina views
* Easy access to shops and services
* Good atmosphere in the evenings

**Cons:**

* Can get busy in the summer
* Parking can be very tricky during peak season

**Good choice if** you want convenience and don’t mind a livelier atmosphere.

**3. Playa del Arenal**

This is Javea’s main sandy beach. It has a long promenade with restaurants and bars, and is popular with both locals and visitors. The area behind the beach has a good mix of apartments and hotels.

**Best for:** Beach lovers, families, and first-time visitors who want easy beach access.

**Pros:**

* Sandy beach with a long promenade
* Plenty of restaurants and amenities nearby
* Good for families

**Cons:**

* Can feel quite touristy in summer
* Parking is limited in high season

**Good choice if** you want a proper beach holiday with everything close by.

**4. Around Cala Granadella**

This is one of the most beautiful areas in Javea. Cala Granadella is a stunning cove with clear water and dramatic cliffs. The surrounding area is quieter and more residential, with some excellent views.

**Best for:** People who want natural beauty, a quieter base, and easy access to one of Javea’s best beaches.

**Pros:**

* Stunning coastal scenery
* Quieter and more peaceful
* Close to Cala Granadella and other coves
* Great for nature lovers and hikers

**Cons:**

* Further from the port and old town
* Limited facilities directly in the area
* Can feel isolated without a car

**Good choice if** you love beautiful beaches and don’t mind being a little further out.



**5. Montgó and Upper Residential Areas**

The areas around Montgó mountain and the higher parts of Javea are more residential and spread out. These are popular with expats and people looking for longer stays.

**Best for:** Longer stays, expats, and anyone wanting peace and quiet with good views.

**Pros:**

* Quieter and more spacious
* Often better value for longer stays
* Nice views and a more local feel
* Usually easier parking

**Cons:**

* Further from the beach and port
* You’ll likely need a car

**Good choice if** you’re staying for a few weeks or more and prefer a calmer environment.

**Quick Recommendations by Type of Stay**

Here’s a quick overview to help you choose the best area in Javea:

* **First-time visitor** — The Puerto or Playa del Arenal are usually the easiest and most convenient choices.
* **Want a quieter beach** — Stay near Cala Granadella for beautiful scenery and a more peaceful atmosphere.
* **Looking for local character** — Javea Old Town offers a more authentic Spanish feel with narrow streets and local restaurants.
* **Love hiking and nature** — The areas around Cala Granadella or Montgó are ideal.
* **Longer stay or winter** — Montgó or the upper residential areas tend to be quieter with better value.
* **Travelling with a dog** — The Old Town, Montgó area, or quieter parts near Cala Granadella usually work well.





**Practical Tips for Booking in Javea**

* **Summer (June–September):** Book early, especially if you want somewhere with parking or sea views.
* **Winter:** Much easier to find good places at better prices. Great time for longer stays.
* **With a dog:** Look for properties that specifically say “pet-friendly”. Many apartments in the Old Town and residential areas are more flexible.
* **Parking:** If you’re renting a car, try to choose accommodation with private parking or easy street parking.
* **Getting around:** Javea is quite spread out. Having a car makes life much easier, especially if you want to visit the coves and hiking trails.

**Sun Sea Sangria Tip:** A good suitcase and a decent daypack make moving around much easier — especially if you plan to explore the different beaches and coves.





Javea has a wonderful variety of areas, each with its own personality. Whether you want beach access, local charm, or peaceful surroundings, there’s a part of Javea that will suit you.
