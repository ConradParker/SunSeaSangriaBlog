Hey Thrill Seekers,

Spring is nearly here, and the **Costa Blanca** is bursting with life—think golden beaches, sunny skies, and that perfect Mediterranean vibe calling your name. If you're planning a trip to the area around **Benidorm**, one absolute must-visit is **Terra Mítica**, the epic theme park that whisks you away on a thrilling journey through ancient Mediterranean civilizations like Egypt, Greece, Rome, Iberia, and the Islands.

This legendary spot combines heart-pounding roller coasters, immersive shows, and family-friendly fun, all set against stunning themed backdrops that make you feel like you've time-traveled. Whether you're chasing adrenaline on massive drops or enjoying spectacular live performances, it's a full-day adventure that's hard to beat on the Costa Blanca.

**When Does Terra Mítica Open in 2026?**

The park kicks off its **2026 season** on **Friday, May 15, 2026**—right in time for those warmer spring days turning into summer vibes. From there, it runs through the season (typically into November, with some special events like Halloween nights).

**Opening hours vary by season:**

**Low season** (e.g., mid-May to late June, and parts of September/October): Usually 10:30 AM to 7:00 PM (or similar, like 10:30–19:00), with access mainly to Egypt, Greece, and Rome areas. **High season** (late June to early September, plus select dates): Extended to around 10:30 AM to 9:00 PM (or 21:00), opening up the full park—including Iberia and the Islands zones for even more rides and experiences. Special nights (like October 31 Halloween) can go late, sometimes until 1:00 AM with themed events.

Always double-check the official calendar closer to your visit, as hours can shift slightly based on the day or weather. The park's official site https://www.terramiticabenidorm.com/en/calendar/ has the most up-to-date info.

**Ticket Prices for 2026**

Prices are tiered by season and whether you buy online (recommended—saves money and skips lines!). Here's the latest breakdown based on current details:

**Low season** (e.g., May–June, September–October periods): Adults (13–64 years): Around €37 online / €41 at the gate. Reduced (kids 4–12 or seniors 65+): Around €32 online / €35 at the gate. Under 4s: Free! **High season** (peak summer months): Adults: Around €45 online / €49 at the gate. Reduced: Often a few euros less accordingly.

Afternoon tickets (from 3 PM onward) or combo deals (with nearby parks like Terra Natura) can offer savings too. Groups (20+ people) get discounts with advance booking. Parking is extra—about €10 for cars at the box office.

Pro tip: Grab tickets online in advance for the best rates and to guarantee entry on busy days. Infants (0–3) enter free with an adult.

**How to Get There**

Terra Mítica sits just outside Benidorm, super accessible from the Costa Blanca hotspots.

**From Alicante Airport (ALC)** — About 55 km away (45–60 minutes drive): **Bus/Shuttle**: Direct options like ALSA buses or shared shuttles (e.g., Beniconnect) to Benidorm, then a short local transfer or taxi to the park. Affordable and straightforward. **Tram/Train combo**: Take the tram from Alicante to Benidorm, then bus or taxi—scenic and budget-friendly. **Private transfer/taxi**: Quickest and most comfortable (around €59–€90+ depending on group size/vehicle). Great if you have luggage or kids. **Rental car**: Easy via the A-7 motorway—direct exit to the park. **From Benidorm center**: Hop on the local bus (like line 1 or others to the train/tram area, then to the park) or grab a quick taxi (around €10–15). Some tour operators run dedicated trips too.

The park has plenty of parking on-site, so driving is hassle-free if you're renting a car for your trip.
