Morning, you lovely lot! If you thought Benidorm was all about 24/7 chaos, endless sunbeds and questionable karaoke… well, you’re half right. But my hubby and I just proved you can smash a cracking 24-hour escape that’s equal parts chill, cheap thrills and proper entertainment—without needing industrial-strength hangover cures the next day. (Well a little )

We rocked up for a cheeky overnight at the **Flash Hotel**—€100 for the night with brekkie included. Bargain or what? Adults-only, smack in the action (hello, two-minute stagger to Levante Beach), and honestly? Spot-on for a quick getaway. Room was clean as a whistle, comfy bed, no nasty surprises. The pool area looks lush—palm trees, sun loungers begging for a siesta but it was a bit chilly, (so maybe next time). But we were too busy being tourists to dive in. Proper value, no faff. If you’re after glamour, look elsewhere; if you want “does what it says on the tin,” Flash nails it.


First stop: straight to **Playa de Levante** for that classic Benidorm skyline hit—you know, the one that makes you go “Blimey, that’s a lot of towers!” Sun was cracking, sea was sparkling, and the promenade was buzzing with just the right amount of energy (March vibes—lively but not elbow-to-elbow madness).

Come dinner time, we went full “when in Benidorm” and hit an **Indian**—very English-style, naturally. Chicken tikka masala, massive naans, the works. None of that fancy fusion nonsense—just comforting, spicy, curry-house nostalgia with a side of “we’re on holiday, who cares?” It was brilliant. Portions big enough to make you waddle happily back to the hotel.

The absolute highlight though? Scooting over to **The Explorers Bar** (or Explorer’s Club—same epic spot) for a **Freddie Mercury tribute show**. Oh. My. Days. The performer was on fire—outfits, moves, voice—all the Queen bangers from “Bohemian Rhapsody” to “We Will Rock You.” Hubby and I were belting along like we were at Wembley ’92. Crowd was buzzing, drinks were cold, and the energy? Electric. Proper feel-good night that left us grinning all the way home. Who knew Benidorm could deliver a tribute act that good? Colour us impressed!

Next morning: hotel brekkie buffet. Busy? Yep, like a Saturday footie crowd. But plenty of everything—bacon, eggs, pastries, fruit, endless coffee. We stuffed our faces, felt suitably smug about the €100 deal, and checked out feeling properly recharged.

So, 24 hours in Benidorm? Turns out it’s not just sun, sea and sangria (though we wouldn’t say no to a pitcher next time). It’s cracking value hotels, beach strolls, balcony views that slap, a proper British curry fix, and a Freddie tribute that’ll have you air-guitaring for days. Affordable, fun, zero regrets.
