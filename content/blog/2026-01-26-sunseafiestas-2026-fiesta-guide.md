The northern Costa Blanca throws the kind of parties that make you wish every week was fiesta week! These lively, heart-pounding celebrations are all about local pride, history, and pure joy—think dazzling costumes, thumping music, streets turned into open-air dance floors, and fireworks that light up the Mediterranean sky. In towns like Jávea, Denia, Altea, Calpe, and Moraira, you’ll catch everything from epic Moros y Cristianos showdowns (Calpe’s version in mid-October is a total spectacle with beach landings, sword fights, and parades that feel like stepping into a movie) to cozy winter bonfires during Sant Antoni (Moraira around mid-January, where locals jump over flames, share hot chocolate, and bless their pets in the cutest processions). Summer brings beachside bangs for Sant Jaume, spring has colorful Easter walks, and quirky gems like Moraira’s “Ugly Day” in late November have everyone dressing in the most ridiculous outfits for laughs and prizes. Best part? Almost every parade, firework display, and street party is completely free to enjoy—just grab a prime spot early, soak up the energy, and let the sangría flow. Dates shift a tiny bit each year, so swing by the local tourist office when you arrive for the exact schedule and insider tips on where to catch the best views. Get ready for nights you'll talk about for years! 🎉🌊🍊
