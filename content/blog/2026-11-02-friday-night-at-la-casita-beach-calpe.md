**Friday Night at La Casita Beach, Calpe**

{{< image-caption src="/images/whatsapp-image-2026-11-02-at-08.34.56-2-.jpeg" >}}Friday Night at La Casita Beach, Calpe{{< /image-caption >}}

It’s been a while since we went out, so Friday night, with the weekend to ourselves, felt like the right moment for a treat. Somewhere seafront, somewhere with a nice view, and somewhere worth making a bit of an effort for (as much effort as a Calpe Friday night usually requires).

That’s how we ended up at La Casita Beach. We sat watching the sun drop over the bay, Calpe’s lights coming on in front of us and Benidorm starting to twinkle in the distance once the sun went down over the mountains. It’s one of those spots that does the coastal thing properly: light, airy, and open enough that you can actually hear the sea instead of just the clatter of cutlery.

The room and the terrace feel bright and easy rather than stiff. No heavy drapes or “fine dining” theatre. Just a lovely seafront place where you can sit for a Friday evening without feeling like you need to whisper.

{{< image-caption src="/images/whatsapp-image-2026-11-02-at-08.34.54-3-.jpeg" >}}Friday Night at La Casita Beach, Calpe{{< /image-caption >}}

My husband ordered the salmon with Béarnaise, chips and salad. I went for the fillet mignon (choice of sauce), and it arrived the same way, chips and salad on the side. The portions are generous without being ridiculous. Both were delicious. The salmon was cooked well, the Béarnaise was buttery (not the thin yellow impostor some places serve), and the steak was tender and well-seasoned. Nothing clever for the sake of it, just good food done right.

Service was one of the best bits of the evening. Our waitress stayed on it the whole time: attentive without hovering, and she kept an eye on the wine without being asked. The kind of attention that makes a Friday night feel looked after instead of rushed.

{{< image-compare src1="/images/whatsapp-image-2026-11-02-at-08.34.55-5-.jpeg" src2="/images/whatsapp-image-2026-11-02-at-08.34.55-1-.jpeg" caption1=" Fillet Mignon (choice of sauce)" caption2="Salmon with Béarnaise," >}}

Price-wise, it sits where you’d expect for a seafront spot in Calpe, mains in the mid-20s to low-30s, so a couple with a bottle of wine and a couple of sides lands comfortably in the €70–90 range depending on what you drink. Not the cheapest table in town, but the portions, the view and the fact that someone actually notices when your glass is empty make it feel fair rather than opportunistic.

If you’re in Calpe and fancy something on the seafront with a lovely view (and room to breathe), La Casita Beach is worth a look. We left full, happy, and already talking about going back.
