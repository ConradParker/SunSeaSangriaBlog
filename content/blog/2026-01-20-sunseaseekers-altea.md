SunSeaSeekers your now in one of my favourite gems of the Costa Blanca!

 It's time to crank the charm dial to max in Altea—the dazzling white pueblo that's often called the most picturesque gem on the Costa Blanca! This bohemian beauty blends hilltop magic with seaside serenity, where whitewashed houses tumble down toward the sparkling Mediterranean like a living painting. Known for its artistic soul, flower-draped streets, and that iconic blue-domed church crowning the skyline, Altea feels like a peaceful escape with just the right spark of excitement. From climbing cobbled lanes for breathtaking views to strolling a palm-lined seafront buzzing with fresh flavours and gentle waves, this town's got that irresistible mix of soul-stirring beauty and laid-back thrills. Let's get lost in it!

**Ascend the Pueblo Blanco: Altea's Enchanting Old Town – Narrow Streets, Art, and Epic Views**

Dive headfirst into **the pueblo** (old town or Casco Antiguo)—Altea's beating heart perched on a hill like a white fortress of dreams! These narrow, winding cobbled streets climb steeply between gleaming whitewashed houses bursting with bougainvillaea, geraniums, and colourful shutters that pop against the blue sky. Every twist reveals hidden plazas, artisan galleries, quirky boutiques selling handmade jewellery, ceramics, and boho treasures—perfect for scoring unique souvenirs that scream "Altea finds"!

The energy builds as you ascend: street musicians strum guitars, cosy cafés spill laughter onto tiny terraces, and the air fills with the scent of fresh coffee and Mediterranean herbs. Pop into a tapas spot for zesty patatas bravas, creamy croquetas, or inventive seafood bites paired with local wines—many spots have killer terraces overlooking the sea. The climb's worth every step for those panoramic payoffs: sweeping vistas of the turquoise bay, distant mountains, and the endless Mediterranean that make your heart skip. It's a romantic, artistic maze where time slows, and every corner feels like pure discovery—get happily lost; it's the best way to feel Altea's creative pulse! Crown the Hill: 

**The Iconic Church and Plaza – Views That Steal Your Breath**

At the summit, the star attraction awaits: the **Parroquia Nuestra Señora del Consuelo**—that famous blue-domed church that's Altea's postcard-perfect symbol! Perched in the charming Plaza de la Iglesia, its twin blue-tiled domes gleam against the sky like Mediterranean jewels, drawing you in with Gothic vibes and a history tied to the town's seafaring past. Step inside for serene coolness and ornate details, then head to the surrounding plaza for the ultimate reward: jaw-dropping 360-degree views over white rooftops, the glittering sea, and far-off cliffs.

Terrace restaurants here turn every meal into magic—sip sangria or fresh juice while the breeze carries sea salt and distant laughter. It's the perfect spot to pause, soak in the panorama, and feel that rush of being on top of the world (literally!). Whether you're snapping epic photos, people-watching locals, or just breathing in the serenity, this hilltop heart delivers goosebumps and unforgettable moments!

**Glide Along the Seafront: Paseo Marítimo – Palm-Lined Promenade, Pebble Beaches, and Coastal Vibes**

Down at sea level, the **Paseo Marítimo** (seafront promenade) is your laid-back playground—a wide, elegant walkway stretching kilometres along the coast, fringed by swaying palms and lapped by crystal-clear waves! This pedestrian paradise blends modern flair with relaxed charm: benches for chilling, shaded spots to rest, and a gentle buzz from families strolling, cyclists gliding by, and the occasional street performer adding melody to the sea breeze.

On one side, pebble beaches sparkle—**Playa de la Roda** (the main urban stretch) mixes white pebbles with sandy patches for easy lounging, swimming in turquoise shallows perfect for snorkelling, or beach volleyball under the sun. Further along, hidden coves and quieter spots like Cap Blanch invite serene dips or paddleboarding adventures. The other side? A lively lineup of fish restaurants, trendy bars, ice-cream parlours, and cafés serving grilled seafood, paella bursting with flavour, and chilled drinks with unbeatable sea views. As the sun dips, the promenade lights up with golden hues, turning sunset walks into pure magic—waves crashing softly, laughter echoing, and that endless feeling of coastal freedom!

There you have it—Altea in all its white-washed, sea-kissed glory! From the pueblo's artistic alleys and hilltop views that make you gasp to the seafront's breezy promenade and pebble-beach bliss, this town's got soul, style, and endless excitement wrapped in Mediterranean magic.
