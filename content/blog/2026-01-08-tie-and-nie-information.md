Hey there, expat adventurers on the sunny Costa Blanca! Let’s talk about **renewing your TIE** (Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero) – that all-important little plastic card that’s your official “I’m legally here” badge in Spain.

As of 2026, the process has gone a bit more digital (thanks to the shiny new Reglamento de Extranjería and platforms like Mercurio), but it’s still mostly about booking that elusive **cita previa**, showing up for fingerprints, and collecting your new card like a prize at the end of a bureaucratic quest.

You can kick off the renewal **60 days before your TIE expires** or up to **90 days after** (though don’t push it too far – fines and frowns might follow). For many folks (especially Brits under the Withdrawal Agreement swapping their 5-year card for a permanent one), it’s super straightforward: no big residency re-application, just a quick fingerprint sesh at the police station.

Grab these essentials:

•  Your expiring TIE and passport

•  Form EX-17 (or EX-23 if you’re a Withdrawal Agreement beneficiary)

•  A recent photo (the exact biometric size, no selfies!)

•  Proof you’ve paid the tasa (Modelo 790-012, around €12-20)

•  Updated padrón if you’ve moved

Book your appointment online via the Sede Electrónica de la Policía Nacional – slots can vanish faster than sangria at a fiesta, so set reminders and refresh like a pro. If you’re tech-savvy with a certificado digital, some renewals (especially non-lucrative or student ones) can start online via Mercurio for less hassle.

Once approved, pop back in about a month to collect your fresh TIE – now often valid longer, like 10 years for permanent ones. Pro tip: while waiting, your resguardo (receipt) plus old TIE keeps you legal inside Spain, but for international travel, you might need an Autorización de Regreso.

There you go – not as scary as it sounds, right? If your specific type (Brexit WA, non-lucrative, etc.) has quirks, double-check locally as provinces love their little twists. Safe renewing, and here’s to many more sunny days with your new card in hand!
