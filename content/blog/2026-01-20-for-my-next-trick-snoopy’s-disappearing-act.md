Picture this: School run done. Kids piled out. One very good (allegedly) Snoopy hops in the back of the car like he owns the place. Windows up, doors shut, engine on. Homeward bound!

Ten minutes later… the back of the car is empty

No dog, no lead, nothing,

No evidence of foul play,

We searched the car like CSI on caffeine.
