**Swapping your UK Driving Licence for a Spanish Driving Licence.**

*(A Red Tape & Tears survival guide — now with fewer actual tears)*

Good news, British expats: swapping your UK driving licence for a Spanish one in 2026 is still refreshingly painless — at least by Spanish bureaucracy standards.

Thanks to the UK–Spain bilateral agreement in force since 16 March 2023, there’s:

*  No theory test
*  No practical test
*  No parallel-parking panic in front of a stone-faced examiner

Just paperwork, a medical check, a fee… and a modest amount of patience.

The process is handled by the **Dirección General de Tráfico (DGT)**, and parts of it can now be started online if you’ve got Cl@ve or a digital certificate. Progress!

## **The Infamous Six-Month Rule (Let’s Unpack It Properly)**

Here’s where people get twitchy.

Once you officially become a Spanish resident — typically marked by the issue date on your TIE card — the clock starts ticking.

From that residency date:

👉 Your valid UK photocard licence is legal for driving in Spain for **6 months**.

After those 6 months:

👉 You cannot legally drive in Spain on your UK licence.

However (deep breath):

* If you’ve booked your exchange appointment before the 6-month deadline, you are generally allowed to continue driving while your application is being processed.
* If you miss the deadline entirely, you can still exchange your licence under the agreement — **no tests required** — but you must stop driving until you receive your Spanish licence or temporary authorisation.

So no, you’re not doomed to retake your test. But yes, the 6-month rule matters.

## **Who Can Exchange?**

You’ll generally qualify if you:

✔ Are a legal Spanish resident
 ✔ Hold a valid UK photocard licence
 ✔ Did not obtain the licence fraudulently after establishing residency

If you still have an old UK paper licence, you’ll usually need to convert it to a photocard via the DVLA before Spain will exchange it.

Standard categories (AM, A1, A2, A, B) are exchanged without tests.
 Heavy vehicle categories (C, D, etc.) may require additional medical controls under Spanish rules.

## **Step-by-Step: The 2026 Survival Checklist**

### **1️⃣ Get Your Medical (Psicotécnico)**

Visit an authorised **Centro de Reconocimiento de Conductores**.

They’ll test:

* Vision
* Reflexes
* General fitness to drive

It usually costs €30–€50, and many centres send results electronically to the DGT.

### **2️⃣ Gather Your Paperwork**

You’ll need:

* Your original UK photocard licence
* Passport
* TIE/proof of residency
* Completed DGT exchange form
* Proof of fee payment (~€28–€30)

Most DGT offices now take your photo digitally, so physical passport photos often aren’t required anymore.

### **3️⃣ Book Your Appointment**

Book a **cita previa** through the DGT website or start the process online if eligible.

Attend your appointment at your local Jefatura Provincial de Tráfico and submit everything.

You’ll surrender your UK licence — it is normally sent back to the UK authorities and not returned to you. When visiting the UK in future, you simply drive on your Spanish licence.

### **4️⃣ Temporary Driving Authorisation**

After approval, the DGT typically issues:

* A temporary driving authorisation valid in Spain
* Usually valid for 3 months (renewable if there are delays)

Your physical Spanish licence card arrives by post a few weeks later.

## **What About Tourists?**

Different rules apply.

UK visitors can generally drive in Spain without needing an International Driving Permit in normal circumstances. But once you become a resident, the 6-month exchange rule applies to you.

Residency is the key trigger.

## **Processing Time**

Varies by province, but typically a few weeks.

If navigating the system makes you want to lie down in a dark room, a gestor can handle it for you — often worth the extra €80–€150 for sanity preservation alone.

## **The Bottom Line**

✔ Do it within 6 months if you can.
 ✔ If you miss it, you can still exchange — just don’t drive meanwhile.
 ✔ No tests required under the current agreement.
 ✔ Once you’ve got your Spanish licence, you’re fully legal and worry-free.

And just like that, you’re officially cleared for coastal drives, mountain switchbacks, and supermarket car parks with terrifyingly tight spaces.

As always, rules can shift, and individual cases vary — so check with the **Dirección General de Tráfico**, a reputable gestor, or official DGT guidance for the latest updates.
