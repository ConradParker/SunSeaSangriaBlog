**SunSeaSangria News** – Marina Alta Norte Vibes |Late May 2026 🌊🍷

Hey SunSeaSangria crew! Another glorious week on the Costa Blanca North, where the sun is shining, the sea is sparkling, and the sangria is flowing just right. Whether you’re a local legend or dreaming of your next escape to Calpe, Altea, Benissa, or Dénia, here’s your laid-back roundup of what’s buzzing in our sunny slice of paradise.

**Beach Life on Point ☀️**

**Big cheers to Calpe – Playa de la Fossa** just hit a legendary milestone with 40 straight years of Blue Flag status! That’s world-class bragging rights for our clean waters, golden sands, and top-notch facilities. They’re throwing a proper celebration on May 30th at Plaza del Mediterráneo – expect good vibes, music, and plenty of reasons to raise a glass. 

The whole stretch is looking fresh: Altea’s charming coves, Benissa’s pristine spots like La Fustera getting extra love for water quality, and Dénia’s beaches flying those Blue Flags high. Perfect conditions for a morning dip followed by a long, lazy lunch.

**Local Life & Good News**

•  Calpe is leveling up accessibility – over 50 local businesses have earned the “Somos Turismo Accesible” badge. More spots welcoming everyone means even better days out. 

•  Mailboxes are popping up in key urbanizations (Carrió, Marisol, etc.) – small win but huge for daily life.

•  Benissa keeping the energy high with youth basketball action and those ongoing pushes for better public services. The education rallies across the Marina Alta (including Dénia and Benissa) show how passionate this community is about the future.

•  Dénia’s gearing up for a massive Festa Major 2026 with tons of live music, tardeos, and that unbeatable port atmosphere. The atún (tuna) spectacle recently was pure gastronomic fire too.

**Your Perfect Weekend Plan**

Grab your towel and your favourite chilled rosé or sangria pitcher. Stroll Altea’s whitewashed old town, hike around the Peñón in Calpe for those epic views, or people-watch in Dénia’s marina while devouring fresh seafood. The weather’s holding steady in the mid-20s with that classic gentle sea breeze – pure magic.

That’s the Marina Alta Norte right now: sun-drenched, community-strong, and always ready for a good time.
