¡Hola from the terrace,

I’ve found myself heading to **Restaurant Vivaldi in Moraira** more and more lately, and I thought it was about time I shared it with you.

It’s one of those places that just feels right, relaxed, friendly, and the food is consistently lovely.

**Where to find it**

You’ll find Vivaldi in Moraira, on the road facing the car park and with beach views. It’s easy to spot and has a lovely outdoor seating area that’s perfect for lingering over lunch or an early evening meal. Great for people watching too.

**The food**

I’ve tried a few different things now and haven’t been disappointed yet.

One of my recent favourites was a generous filled pitta shawarma with tender, well-seasoned pork meat, served with a big portion of crispy chips and a couple of tasty sauces on the side. Simple, satisfying, and exactly what you want when you’re hungry.

My husband also had a beautiful tuna tartare dish, fresh, colourful, and full of flavour, with avocado, mango and a light dressing. It looked as good as it tasted and felt like a little holiday on a plate.

Everything comes out looking carefully put together without being pretentious. Portions are good, the flavours are clean, and it all feels fresh.

### **The vibe**

What I really like about Vivaldi is the atmosphere. It’s relaxed enough that you can turn up in shorts and flip-flops, but the food and presentation still feel special. Service has always been friendly and unhurried when we have been there.

It’s the kind of place you can go for a nice, easy-going lunch with friends or a quieter meal when you just want something nice without any fuss.

**Final thoughts**

If you’re in or around Moraira and fancy somewhere reliable with good food and a pleasant setting, Vivaldi is well worth adding to your list. It’s become one of my favourites for a reason.
