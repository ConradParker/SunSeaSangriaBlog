Summer is slowly winding down on the Costa Blanca North, even though it’s only mid-August. You could see it clearly this weekend – cars packed to the roof, suitcases strapped on, and a steady stream of tourists heading for the AP-7 and Alicante airport. The weather is still properly hot (those muggy 33–35 °C days with high humidity), but the mornings and evenings are just starting to feel a little cooler. The shift is coming.

What’s been going on around Costa Blanca North this past week

Calpe put on a real show. The Calpe Pop Festival wrapped up three nights at the Parque de la Creatividad and the Saturday closer featured God Save The Queen – one of the best Queen tributes around – belting out all the classics, including a stomping We Will Rock You. Locals and visitors loved it; by all accounts it went down an absolute storm and made the perfect finale to Calpe’s August fiestas.

Dénia has been deep in its Moros y Cristianos celebrations. The spectacular Gala Parade filled the streets on Saturday and today (Sunday 16 August) brings the traditional arquebus battle, the Miracle of the Fog, the Sant Roc procession and the final retreta. Colour, music, gunpowder and that classic Marina Alta fiesta atmosphere right up to the last shot.

Altea has been quieter after the big Castell de l’Olla fireworks earlier in the month, though there was a midweek paddle-surf rescue off the coast by Cruz Roja – a timely reminder that even calm mornings can catch people out.

Benidorm, El Campello, Jávea and Alicante stayed in full high-summer mode: busy beaches, packed promenades, open-air cinema nights and smaller local verbenas. Jávea is still enjoying the afterglow of its Jazz Festival, while El Campello and Alicante city beaches made the most of the settled weather once the alerts lifted.

Jalón Valley (Xaló and surrounding villages) had a sharp midweek reminder of how quickly the weather can change inland. A short, intense storm dumped a good amount of rain across Xaló, Parcent and nearby spots, with strong winds and some localised flooding. The valley is looking green again and life has settled back into its usual peaceful rhythm of vineyards, almond groves and quiet village mornings.

What’s on this weekend & looking ahead in Costa Blanca North

The big fiestas are winding down, so the pace eases a little – which many of us won’t mind. Expect the usual Sunday markets, beach cinema (Dénia is still running films on the sand), and plenty of open-air music and food events as towns ease into late August. Keep an eye on local boards for last-minute verbenas or smaller concerts – there’s always something popping up between Calpe, Altea, Jávea and Dénia.

Weather-wise we’re staying warm and sunny by day, but those cooler edges in the morning and evening should become more noticeable as the week goes on. Perfect for early walks or late terrace dinners with a glass of something cold.
