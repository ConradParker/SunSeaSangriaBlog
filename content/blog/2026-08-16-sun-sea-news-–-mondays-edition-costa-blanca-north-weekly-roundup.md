¡Hola & Good Morning from the Costa Blanca North Terrace! 

Summer is starting to ease its grip, even though we’re only mid-August. You could feel it this weekend — cars loaded to the roof, suitcases strapped on, and a steady stream of holidaymakers heading for the AP-7 and Alicante airport. The days are still very hot (those sticky 33–35 °C numbers with the humidity cranked up), but the mornings and evenings are just beginning to soften. The shift is coming.

**What’s been going on around Costa Blanca North this past week**

Calpe put on a fantastic show over the weekend. The Calpe Pop Festival wrapped up three nights at the Parque de la Creatividad, and Saturday’s closer featured God Save The Queen, one of the best Queen tributes around, belting out all the classics, including a stomping *We Will Rock You*. Locals and visitors loved every minute of it. By all accounts, it was the perfect finale to Calpe’s August fiestas.

**Dénia** has been deep in its Moros y Cristianos celebrations. The spectacular Gala Parade filled the streets on Saturday, and today (Sunday 16th) brings the traditional arquebus battle, the Miracle of the Fog, the Sant Roc procession and the final retreta. Colour, music, gunpowder and that classic Marina Alta fiesta atmosphere right up to the last shot.

**Altea** has been quieter after the big Castell de l’Olla fireworks earlier in the month, though there was a midweek paddle-surf rescue off the coast by Cruz Roja — a timely reminder that even calm mornings can catch people out.

**Benidorm**, **El Campello, Jávea and Alicante** stayed in full high-summer mode: busy beaches, packed promenades, open-air cinema nights and smaller local verbenas. Jávea is still enjoying the afterglow of its Jazz Festival, while El Campello and Alicante city beaches made the most of the settled weather once the alerts lifted.

Over in the **Jalón Valley** (Xaló and surrounding villages) there was a sharp midweek reminder of how quickly the weather can change inland. A short, intense storm dumped a good amount of rain across Xaló, Parcent and nearby spots, with strong winds and some localised flooding. The valley is looking green again and life has settled back into its usual peaceful rhythm of vineyards, almond groves and quiet village mornings.

**What’s on this weekend & looking ahead**

The big fiestas are winding down, so the pace eases a little — which many of us won’t mind one bit. Expect the usual Sunday markets, beach cinema (Dénia is still running films on the sand), and plenty of open-air music and food events as towns ease into late August. Keep an eye on local boards for last-minute verbenas or smaller concerts — there’s always something popping up between Calpe, Altea, Jávea and Dénia.

**Weather-**wise we’re staying warm and sunny by day, but those cooler edges in the morning and evening should become more noticeable as the week goes on. Perfect for early walks or late terrace dinners with a glass of something cold. Watch out on **Wednesday** when those sneaky weather alerts for high temputures come back.
