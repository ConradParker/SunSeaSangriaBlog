Pull up a lounger. This one’s a bit of an update and hopefully for a long time,

I saw the traumatologist last Monday. I told him straight that if I’d gone at the beginning of October, I probably wouldn’t have thought the PRP had done much. It was still sore, still limiting, and I was starting to wonder if it had been a waste of time and money.

Then, somewhere around mid-October, things started to shift. I could manage longer walks without the same level of protest from the hip. It’s not fixed, and I'm not doing marathons. My hip and thigh are still sore, and putting on socks is still a silly performance. Some days it feels like it’s slipped backwards again. But overall, and slowly, it does feel better than it did.

I’m gardening again, which I love, and I’m walking more. I’m not 100%, not even close, but I’m able to do more of the ordinary things without constantly negotiating with the joint. The doctor was pleased. He said this is exactly why he trained in PRP: to see people get their normal life back without going straight to something more invasive.

So that’s where I am. Taking it day by day, trying to move a bit more when I can, and not assuming it’s all sorted. I’ll keep you posted if anything changes, for better or worse.

That’s the update from the terrace.
