**¡Buenos días desde la Costa Blanca! 🌞☕🍊 Breakfast in Spain: The Sunny Start You Didn’t Know You Needed**

**Morning, are you ready to rise & shine?**. 

If you’ve ever wondered why everything feels just that little bit more magical here in Spain, let me let you in on a secret: it starts with breakfast. Forget rushed bowls of cereal or massive fry-ups (though we love a good English brekkie now and then 😉). Spanish desayuno is all about simple pleasures, fresh flavours, and easing into the day with sunshine on your plate.

Whether you’re perched at a beachside café in Calpe watching the waves roll in, or grabbing a quick bite in Javea, breakfast here is light, affordable, and oh-so-Spanish. Let’s dive in, shall we? Grab your virtual cup of orange juice (freshly squeezed, naturally) and come along… 🏖️🍷

**The Classic: Tostada con Tomate y Aceite (and Maybe a Little Jamón)**
This is the undisputed king of Spanish breakfasts – and once you try it, you’ll never look back.
Crusty bread toasted just right, rubbed with ripe tomato (or grated straight onto the toast for that juicy magic), drizzled with silky extra virgin olive oil, and a sprinkle of sea salt. Add thin slices of jamón ibérico if you’re feeling fancy (or just because it’s Spain and why not?).

It’s fresh, healthy-ish, and ridiculously moreish. Pair it with a café con leche (milky coffee that’s basically a hug in a cup) and a zumo de naranja natural (fresh orange juice – Valencia oranges are basically liquid sunshine). Under €5 for the lot in most places. Bliss.

Many cafés have it. Scrambled eggs with extras, or even a cheeky full English if you're missing home – it's all on offer, especially around El Campello's beachfront spots and tourist cafés. Mix Spanish simplicity with your own twist... no judgments here! 😏
