**From early-bird breakfasts to those long, lazy Mediterranean brunches—and every delicious bite in between.**

If there is one thing we believe in at ***Sun Sea Sangria***, it’s that brunch is not just a meal—it’s a lifestyle. And during my latest trip to Calpe, I found the ultimate headquarters for it: Mimosa Brunch.

Nestled in the heart of this coastal town, Mimosa Brunch manages to strike that perfect balance between a chic, modern brunch spot and a warm, welcoming local haunt. Whether you’re recovering from a night of sangria or fuelling up for a hike up the Peñón de Ifach, this is the place to be.

**The Vibe: Coastal Chic meets Relaxed Indulgence**

From the moment you walk in, you’re greeted by an "Ibiza-meets-the-Mainland" aesthetic. Think botanical touches, plenty of sunlight, and a soundtrack that makes you want to stay for a second (or third) coffee. It’s the kind of place where the staff treat you like a regular on your first visit, which instantly earns them a spot on our "Sun Sea Foodie" favourites list.

**What’s on the Plate?**

The menu is a dream for anyone who loves a mix of classic breakfast staples and Instagram-worthy creations. Here’s what stood out:

* The Classics Done Right: From perfectly scrambled eggs to the perfect tacos, they don't cut corners on quality.
* The Artisan Touch: Their toasts are game-changers—especially when topped with fresh avocado or salty Iberian ham.
* Sweet Treats: If you have a sweet tooth, their fluffy pancakes are a must-try. Pair them with a fresh orange juice (it’s the Costa Blanca, after all!) or a beautifully crafted latte art coffee.

**Why We Love It**

What makes La Mimosa special isn't just the food—it’s the pace. It’s a "slow living" kind of spot where nobody rushes you. You can sit back, soak in the Calpe sun, and truly savour the flavours.

Sun Sea Sangria Tip: It’s a popular spot for a reason! If you’re heading there on a weekend, try to get there a little earlier to snag the best table before they go, or choose a lovely spot on their lovely outdoor terrace.

**Blog Sidebar / Quick Info:**

* Best for: Boozy brunches, lazy mornings, and coffee lovers.
* Must Order: The Eggs Benedict or any of the Mimosas (obviously!).
* Vibe: 10/10 welcoming, good food and lovely staff.
