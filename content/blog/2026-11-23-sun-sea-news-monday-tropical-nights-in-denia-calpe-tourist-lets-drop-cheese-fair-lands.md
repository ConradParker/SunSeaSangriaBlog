Hola Terrace Crew,

Weekends go by quicker than a Formula 1 car these days, don’t they? Did you catch the Las Vegas Grand Prix? Great racing and more overtakes than I’ve seen in years. Made for a decent Sunday on the sofa.

**Elections on the 29th (yes, this Sunday)**

Campaign posters are everywhere from Alicante city up through Villajoyosa, Benidorm, Altea, Calpe, Xàbia and into Dénia. A fresh poll this morning has the PP looking strong in Alicante province (around five seats in one survey, four in another), with Vox closing the gap on the PSOE. Locally the Marina Alta remains a battleground between the progressive side and the right. Dénia even got a detailed street-by-street historical vote map published this week if you fancy a deep dive. Practical note: if you’re registered and haven’t checked your polling station yet, do it sooner rather than later – the ayuntamientos have online tools.

**Dénia’s big northern expansion**

The big local talking point is the Playa-Torrecremada / Ronda Norte sector. The council has pushed forward a plan that could eventually bring up to roughly 1,888 homes, with a substantial share of protected/affordable housing, plus a school, parking and green space. Buildings are capped under 30 metres so the castle view isn’t blocked. There’s already pushback from developers’ groups arguing the tender documents didn’t fully account for the current national housing law’s higher protected-housing percentage, which changes the numbers. Classic Costa Blanca urbanism story – ambitious on paper, messy in the detail. If you live near the port access or the northern edge of town, keep an eye on it.

**Everyday stuff that actually matters**

•  The lift at the CSI La Pedrera health centre in Dénia has been out of action for 14 months. Sanidad has finally put the replacement out to tender (north of €80,000). About time.

•  Dénia is hiring four museum security guards. No university degree required, but you do need the Valencian language qualification. Three tests, two of them eliminatory. Useful if you or someone you know is looking for a steady public-sector role.

•  In Els Poblets (still recovering from the earlier DANA), a solidarity festival helped raise just over €45,000 for those affected. Meanwhile mayors from across the political spectrum in the Marina Alta have joined forces to demand proper flood-prevention investment so the same thing doesn’t keep happening.

**Culture and the odd rescue**

Dénia hosted the Premis Ovidi gala celebrating music in Valencian – good turnout and the usual mix of local pride and emotional moments. Over in Calpe there was a helicopter rescue of a climber with a serious leg injury on the Peñón de Ifach earlier in the season (the rock is beautiful but unforgiving). Teachers across the comarca, including a concentration today in Xàbia, continue protesting classroom ratios and safety issues.

**Quick positives and practicals**

The posidonia meadows off Dénia are in generally good shape according to the latest monitoring (no invasive algae spotted). Tourism accommodation numbers have dropped sharply in Dénia, Xàbia, Calpe and Teulada – whether that translates into more long-term rental stock remains to be seen. The coast is still gorgeous, the sea is calm, and the light is perfect for a late-afternoon walk whether you’re in Altea, Benissa or up in Dénia’s old town.

That’s your Monday snapshot from Alicante to Dénia. If something specific is happening in your town (Villajoyosa, La Vila, Calpe, Xàbia or wherever) that I missed, shout,  happy to dig further next week.
