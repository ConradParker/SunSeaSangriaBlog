Rainy days on the **Costa Blanca North** (Calpe, Javea/Xàbia, Altea, Moraira, Benissa, Alfaz del Pi, and up to Benidorm vibes) don’t have to mean staying cooped up—especially since showers are usually short and the mild temps make indoor escapes easy. Here are some top **indoor-friendly places** perfect for expats, families, or anyone wanting to dodge the drizzle. These focus on the northern stretch, with easy drives from Calpe (most under 30-45 mins).

**1. Shopping Centres – The Ultimate Rain-Proof Hangout**

•  **La Marina Shopping Centre** (Ondara, ~30 mins from Calpe): Huge, shopping centre with tons of shops like Zara, Lefties, Pull and Bear, Berksha , Restaurants, Cinema IMF  Check Website for English Speaking Movies VO, and young  kid-friendly zones. Great for a full day out.

•  **Gran Vía or similar in nearby areas** (e.g., closer to Benidorm/Altea): Cinema with English speaking movies (VO), Childrens Club, Gran Via Kids, Trampolines and small rides , leisure, and cafes under one roof.



**2. Indoor Play & Adventure Centres – Kid Heaven**

•  **J-Park Indoor Play Centre** (Calpe area): Right on your doorstep—soft play, climbing, games for little ones.

•  **Airjump Trampoline Park** (Finestrat, near Benidorm): Bouncing fun for all ages, plus some soft play.

•  **Street Jump** (Elche, bit further south but worth it): Massive trampoline park + soft play + inflatables—top-rated for families on rainy days.

**3. Museums & Cultural Spots – Educational & Dry**

•  **Museo Casal del Fester** (Altea): Small, fascinating museum on Moors & Christians fiestas—colorful costumes and history.

•  **Other nearby gems**: Ethnic Music Museum (Busot area), or Paleontological Museum (Elche) for dinosaur fans—great for kids.

•  In Benidorm: Chocolate Museum or similar quirky indoor spots.

**4. Bowling, Arcades & Laser Tag – Fun Group Activities**

•  Bowling alleys scattered around (Benidorm, La Nucía areas): Classic games + arcades + food.

•  **Megazone** or similar: Laser tag, indoor paintball for older kids/teens.

**5. Other Cozy Options**

•  **Spa/Wellness Centres**: Heated indoor pools, saunas, or massages in local spots (Benidorm has plenty).

•  **Live Music Cafés or Artisan Shops**: Browse ceramics/jewelry in Altea or Moraira boutiques—peaceful and covered.

•  **Cinema or Covered Markets**: Catch a movie or wander indoor markets.

Pro tip: Rain here is often patchy—check forecasts; if it’s light, a quick umbrella walk in Altea’s old town or Moraira’s harbor can still be magical. Pair with a hot chocolate or tapas stop!

These keep the day fun and dry without straying far from Calpe.
