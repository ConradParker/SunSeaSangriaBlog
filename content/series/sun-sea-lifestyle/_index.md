**Welcome to SunSea Lifestyle: Real Life on the Costa Blanca North**

Hey there, fellow sun-chasers, villa-dwellers and newbies dreaming of that endless Mediterranean glow!

If you’ve landed here, chances are you’re either already sipping **sangria** with sea views or seriously plotting your escape to the Costa Blanca North – that glorious, slightly less hectic stretch where the mountains kiss the Med, the beaches stay golden year-round, and life moves at a pace that actually lets you enjoy it.

**SunSea Lifestyle** is your no-fluff, zero-BS companion to living here full-time in 2026. Not the glossy holiday brochure version with infinity pools and endless sunsets (though we’ve got plenty of those too). This is the real deal: the everyday wins, the cheeky hacks, the “why didn’t anyone tell me that?” moments that turn a dream move into actual, brilliant daily life.

We’re talking:

•  How to actually stock your fridge without blowing the budget (because yes, even paradise has a weekly shop).

•  Mastering the local rhythm – from siesta survival to finding the best fresh produce without turning into a tourist.

•  Villa life hacks, hidden beaches, mountain walks that beat the heat, and where to grab that perfect bottle of local wine for under a tenner.

•  The honest bits: healthcare that actually works, making friends (expats and locals), and keeping costs sane when eggs have decided to throw a tantrum this year.

We live it, we laugh about it, and we share it with that cheeky grin – because life up here in the North is bloody good, but it’s even better when you know the shortcuts.

First up in the series: **Supermarket Showdown – Who’s Winning Your Trolley in Costa Blanca North 2026**. Mercadona still wearing the crown for most of us? Are Lidl and Aldi keeping more euros in your pocket for sunset Aperols? And why does Más y Más make you feel a bit more “proper local”? We break it down with real vibes, current prices, and zero corporate fluff.

Whether you’re already here with your flip-flops permanently on, or still googling “Costa Blanca cost of living” at 2am, pull up a chair (or a sun lounger), grab a cold drink, and join the gang.

This is SunSea Lifestyle – where the sea sparkles, the sangria flows, and we keep it real about making this slice of Spain your everyday home.

Drop a comment, tell us what you want covered next (British sausages emergency? Best beach bars? Remote working setups?), and let’s make the most of this sunshine-soaked life together.
