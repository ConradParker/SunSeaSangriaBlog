**Looking Through the Looking Glass** 

Bringing p you a focused weekly roundup of **Costa Blanca North** news – tailored for expats, long-term residents, and holidaymakers enjoying Calpe, Javea, Moraira, Altea, Benidorm, Benissa and the surrounding gems. 

Living right here in Calpe, I know what hits home: from property market insights (penthouses and coastal buys still popular among internationals) and rental regs, to community stories (support for local causes like Project 4 All), transport passes, blue-flag beaches, local fiestas, healthcare access, tax tips for pensioners, and weather/travel alerts. We skip the national noise and zoom in on our patch – practical, English-language updates delivered weekly to help you navigate, plan, and enjoy life on this sun-drenched coast.
