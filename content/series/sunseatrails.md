Hello, you lovely lot!

If you’ve been following along, you’ll know we’re all about squeezing every drop of joy from this gorgeous stretch of Costa Blanca North — the beaches, the fiestas, the food, the faffing with paperwork (and surviving it with a strong cuppa). But let’s be honest: sometimes you just need to get out there, feel the sun on your face, and let the salty Med breeze blow away the cobwebs.

Welcome to **SunSeaTrails** — your cheeky little guide to the best walks, hikes, and proper leg-stretchers around Jávea, Altea, Denia, and beyond. We’re talking hidden coves with views that make you go “blimey,” coastal paths where the sea’s practically close enough to dip your toes mid-stride, mountain tracks with pine-scented air, and the odd cheeky scramble that earns you an extra-large tinto de verano at the end.

No fancy gear required (though decent trainers and a hat are non-negotiable in this heat). We’ll keep it real: easy strolls for when the knees are feeling their age, moderate wanders perfect for a morning out with the dog, and the occasional proper hike that’ll leave you glowing (and maybe a bit sweaty). Expect honest tips — parking nightmares, best times to avoid the crowds, dog-friendly bits, what snacks to stuff in your backpack, and of course, where to grab a cold beer or sangria afterwards to toast your aching calves.

Because life here isn’t just about lounging (though we do love a good lounger moment). It’s about getting out, breathing it all in, and coming home with sandy toes, a camera full of views, and stories to tell over tapas.

So grab your water bottle, slather on the factor 50, and come along for the walk. The Costa Blanca’s trails are calling — and they’re far too pretty to ignore.

¡Salud, and see you on the path! 🍹🥾
