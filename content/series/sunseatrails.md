**Welcome to Sun Sea Hikes &** Trails — your cheeky little guide to the best walks, hikes, and proper leg-stretchers around Jávea, Altea, Denia, and beyond. We’re talking hidden coves with views that make you go “blimey,” coastal paths where the sea’s practically close enough to dip your toes mid-stride, mountain tracks with that lovely pine-scented air, and the odd cheeky scramble that earns you a great glass of sangria at the end.

No fancy gear required (though decent trainers and a hat are non-negotiable in this heat). We’ll keep it real: easy strolls for when the knees are feeling their age (trust me I know ), moderate wanders perfect for a morning out with the dog, and the occasional proper hike that’ll leave you glowing (and maybe a bit knackered). Expect honest tips — parking nightmares, best times to dodge the crowds, dog-friendly spots, what snacks to chuck in your rucksack, and of course, where to grab a cold beer or sangria afterwards to toast your aching calves.

Because life here isn’t just about lounging (though we do love a good lounger moment). It’s about getting out, breathing it all in, and coming home with sandy toes, a camera full of cracking views, and stories to tell over tapas.

So grab your water bottle, slap on the factor 50, and come along for the walk. The Costa Blanca’s trails are calling — and they’re far too pretty to ignore.

¡Salud, and see you on the path! 🍹🥾
