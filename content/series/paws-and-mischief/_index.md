Meet Snoopy, our pint-sized rescue dog who crash-landed into our lives straight from a little town in Andalucía.

His previous owner, a lovely lady who had fallen ill, simply couldn’t bear to say goodbye to him. A friend who lived in the mountains began a campaign of daily dog emojis, cute photos, and pleading texts: “He’s tiny! He loves kids! Look at that face!”

We lasted about a week before caving. “Fine,” we said. “How much trouble can one small dog be?”

(Spoiler: a lot. The best kind.)

Somewhere between the chaos and the cuddles, this little Spanish rescue completely rearranged our home and our hearts.

*Paws and Mischief* is the true story of how one tiny dog from Andalucía taught us that the best kind of trouble makes life bigger, louder, and full of unexpected joy.

If you’ve ever fallen for a rescue, survived a small-dog takeover, or just need a feel-good dose of four-legged mischief, this one’s for you.
