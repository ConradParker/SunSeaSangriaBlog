**Our Series – Your Costa Blanca North Terrace Companions**

**Pull up a lounger and stay a while.**

Here you’ll find all the stories, tips, adventures and cheeky moments that make life on the Costa Blanca North so special. Whether you’re dreaming of moving here, planning your next holiday, or already living the dream, there’s a series just for you.

Browse by what makes your heart happy — and don’t forget to join the Terrace Crew for updates!

{{< image-caption src="/images/c6r3v.jpg" >}}{{< /image-caption >}}

**[🌊 Sun Sea Lifestyle](https://sunseasangria.com/series/sun-sea-lifestyle/)**

***Real talk about expat life on the Costa Blanca North***

Honest, heart-warming stories about what it’s actually like to build a life here. From making Spanish friends and adjusting to “mañana” time, to the everyday magic of terrace living, winter sun, and turning this beautiful coast into home. Perfect if you’re thinking of moving or want to feel less alone on the journey.

**[🍽️ Sun Sea Foodie](https://sunseasangria.com/series/sunseafoodie/)**

***Local flavours, hidden tapas bars & proper Spanish eating***

Where to find the best arroz dishes, freshest market produce, sunset sangria jars, and those secret chiringuitos the locals love. Expect restaurant recommendations, simple terrace recipes, and market haul inspiration from the Marina Alta.

**[🗺️ Sun Sea Seekers](https://sunseasangria.com/series/sss-town-guide/)**

***Hidden gems and must-do places in the Marina Alta***

Your friendly guide to secret coves, mountain villages, dramatic viewpoints and authentic experiences that go far beyond the tourist trail. Whether you’re here for a week or forever, these posts help you explore like a local.

**[🥾 Sun Sea Hikes & Trails](https://sunseasangria.com/series/sunseatrails/)**

***Beautiful walks for the cooler months***

Real, doable routes across the Costa Blanca North — from gentle coastal paths with sea views to proper mountain adventures. Each hike includes difficulty level, best time to go, GPX tips, and where to stop for a well-earned café con leche or beer.

**[📬 Sun Sea News](https://sunseasangria.com/series/sun-sea-news/)**

***Every Monday – What’s really happening locally***

Your warm weekly round-up of Marina Alta news: new restaurants opening, fiesta dates, important updates for expats, beach projects, and the good stuff that actually matters to those of us who live here.

**[📚 Sun Sea Books](https://sunseasangria.com/series/the-book-club/)**

***Our friendly online book club with a Spanish twist***

Monthly book picks, author spotlights (especially local writers), discussion prompts, and perfect reads for the beach or terrace. Join fellow book lovers who understand the pull of the Mediterranean.

**[📋 Red Tape & Tears](https://sunseasangria.com/series/red-tape-tears/)**

***No-nonsense help with Spanish paperwork***

The practical guides we all need. Clear, up-to-date advice on NIE, SIP, TIE, padrón, gestors, and everything else. Includes our popular free downloadable Expat Starter Pack to make the process less stressful.

**[🎉 Fiestas & Sangria](https://sunseasangria.com/series/fiestas-sangria/)**

***Celebrations, traditions & proper Spanish fun***

From spectacular Moros y Cristianos parades to intimate village fiestas and wild Hogueras nights. We show you when they happen, what to expect, and how to join in like a local — always with a glass of sangria in hand.

**[🐶 Paws & Mischief](https://sunseasangria.com/series/test-story/)**

***Heart warming and cheeky stories starring Snoopy***

The ongoing adventures of our beloved dog Snoopy and his four-legged friends on the Costa Blanca. Funny, emotional, and full of joy — celebrating the animals that make expat life so much richer.
