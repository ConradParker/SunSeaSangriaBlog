Welcome to the brand-new **SunSeaSangria Book Club** – where we dive into captivating novels written by expats, all with a glass of sangria in hand (real or imaginary)! If you love stories that blend the thrill of life abroad with the rich flavours of Spanish culture, this is the place for you. We’re kicking things off with member-driven picks, so please share your favourite expat-authored Spanish books – we’d love to add them to our reading list! Or if you have written a book and want to share it with us, that would be wonderful. Join us for lively discussions, personal reviews, and thoughtful reflections on each month’s selection. Whether you’re lounging on a Spanish beach or dreaming of one from afar, come read, sip, and connect with fellow book lovers. Sign up on the blog or drop a comment to get involved and lets get reading.
